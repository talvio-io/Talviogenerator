What is Talvio Generator?

10,000 Black Dot robot NFTs on BNB Chain. Four generators — Prime, Pixel Dot, Stealth Dot and Aurum Dot — each waving hello, with mixed backgrounds, shells and dot cores across the supply.

How much does it cost?

Your first 2 NFTs per wallet are free (you only pay network gas). After that, 0.002 BNB per mint, max 20 paid mints per wallet.

When does minting open?

Mint opens the moment the Talvio Generator contract is deployed — this page reads the contract live and lights up automatically.

Can I mint with a Binance agent wallet?

Yes. Connect with your browser wallet for one-click minting, or copy the agent prompt in the mint section and send it to an AI agent with a Binance Agent ID wallet.

No. The contract is a plain ERC-721: no mint tax, no transfer fee, no blacklist. What you mint is yours.
